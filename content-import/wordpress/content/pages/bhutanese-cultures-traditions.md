## Bhutanese Cuisine

Ema Datshi is the national dish of Bhutan, beloved for its fiery flavor, simplicity, and cultural significance. The name itself comes from the Dzongkha language: “Ema” means chili, and “Datshi” means cheese. So, at its core, Ema Datshi is a dish made from chilies and cheese—a combination that perfectly reflects the Bhutanese love for bold flavors and locally sourced ingredients.

## Mask Dance

One of the most captivating aspects of Bhutanese culture is the mask dance, locally known as Cham. Performed during tsechus (religious festivals) in dzongs and monasteries, Cham dances are a spectacular blend of spirituality, storytelling, and cultural preservation. They are more than just entertainment—they are a living expression of Bhutan’s Buddhist heritage and moral teachings.
