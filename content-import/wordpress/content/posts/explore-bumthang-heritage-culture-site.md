# Explore Bumthang heritage & culture site

Bumthang is the spiritual heartland of Bhutan, consisting of four high-altitude valleys: Choekhor, Tang, Chumey, and Ura. Sightseeing Explore key sites in Bumthang including Membar Tso (a sacred lake), Tamshing Lhakhang known for ancient wall paintings, Kurje Lhakhang, Jampa Lhakhang—one of Bhutan’s oldest temples, Khonchoksum Lhakhang, and Kharchu Monastery.

## Kurjey Lhakhang སྐུ་རྗེས་ལྷ་ཁང་།

![](https://lotuspeak.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/kurji-lhakhang-1024x768.webp)

One of Bhutan’s most sacred sites.

* **Travel Info**: Located in the Choekhor valley, it is easily accessible by car or a scenic walk from Jakar town.

* **Significance**: It marks the spot where Guru Rinpoche meditated in the 8th century and left his body imprint on a rock, which is now preserved inside the oldest of the three temples.

## Tamshing Lhakhang གཏམ་ཞིང་ལྷ་ཁང་།

![Tamshing-Lhakhang](https://lotuspeak.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Tamshing-Lhakhang.webp)

Founded in 1501 by the great treasure revealer Terton Pema Lingpa.

* Tamshing Lhakhang is situated in Bumthang Valley, offering breathtaking views of surrounding landscapes, including the Kurjey Lhakhang across the river. Its serene environment enhances visitors’ spiritual experiences.
* **Significance**: It contains ancient murals painted by Pema Lingpa himself that have never been restored. A notable feature is a heavy iron chain vest forged by the saint; tradition says walking around the inner sanctuary three times while wearing it cleanses one’s sins.
* **Travel Info**: Situated across the river from Kurjey Lhakhang.

## Highlights include:

* Its remarkable murals from the 16th century that vividly depict Buddhist teachings, offering visitors a glimpse into Bhutan’s artistic heritage.
* The unique heavy iron chain made by Pema Lingpa himself, which pilgrims carry on their shoulders for spiritual purification. It is an authentic ritual appreciated by many.
* The serene atmosphere within the ancient complex, where quiet reflection is often complemented by interactions with dedicated monks.

> “Bumthang, The is the spiritual heartland of Bhutan.”

## Könchogsum Lhakhang དཀོན་མཆོག་གསུམ་ལྷ་ཁང་།

![koenchosum-lhakhang](https://lotuspeak.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/koenchosum-lhakhang.webp)

Originally built in the 8th or 9th century and recently restored after a major fire in 2010.

**Travel Info**: Located just a short walk below Tamshing Lhakhang.

**Significance**: It houses three statues representing the past, present, and future Buddhas, which legend says flew there from the east. It also features a large, cracked bell that is said to be audible as far as Tibet when rung.

## **Membartsho** (Dzongkha མེ་འབར་མཚོ།)

**Membartsho** (Dzongkha མེ་འབར་མཚོ།), also known as **Mebar Tsho** or **Burning Lake**, is a holy site, revered as the place where Pema Lingpa, Bhutan’s greatest tertön (treasure revealer), discovered several of Guru Rinpoche’s terma in the 15th century. It is a 30-minute drive from Chamkhar town and takes around five minutes from the road end to the lake

![](https://lotuspeak.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/mebar-tsho-1024x683.jpeg)

A sacred pool in the Tang Chhu river.

* **Significance**: Legend states that Pema Lingpa dove into the lake with a burning butter lamp and emerged with sacred treasures and the lamp still lit.
* **Travel Info**: A 30-minute drive from Jakar town toward Tang valley, followed by a short 5-minute walk down to the riverbank.

## Tharpaling Monastery ཐར་པ་གླིང་ལྷ་ཁང་།

Known as the “Place of Liberation,” situated at 3,600m.

* **Significance**: Founded in the 14th century by the Tibetan master Longchen Rabjam, it is a renowned meditation and retreat center. It is famous for its massive seven-foot stone phallus, the “Great Cannon of Tharpaling,” erected to ward off negative spirits.
* **Travel Info**: Accessible by a 50-minute drive (approx. 22 km) through a winding dirt road from the village of Gaytsa. For 2026, the **Tharpaling Tshechu** festival is scheduled for **March 3, 2026**

![tharpaling](https://lotuspeak.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/tharpaling-1024x683.webp)

## 2026 Visitor Guidelines

* **Logistics**: Bumthang is reachable via a domestic flight from Paro (approx. 35 mins) or a full day’s drive from Thimphu.

* **Entry Fees**: Most major temples and monasteries in Bumthang now follow the standard national monument fee of **Nu 500** for adults and **Nu 250** for children (6–17 years).

## Kharchu Dratshang མཁར་ཆུ་གྲྭ་ཚང་།

Kharchu Dratshang is a significant Buddhist monastery located in Jakar, Bhutan. It serves as a major center for monastic education and spiritual practice in the region. The monastery is known for its traditional Bhutanese architecture and serene environment, which attracts both pilgrims and tourists.

![](https://lotuspeak.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/kurji-lhakhang.webp)

A major seat of the Nyingma school founded in the 1970s.

* **Travel Info**: Perched on a hilltop overlooking Jakar town, offering some of the best panoramic views of the valley and Jakar Dzong
* **Significance**: It is a vibrant center for Buddhist learning, housing over 400 monks. It is particularly known for its preservation of the teachings of ***Namkhai Nyingpo Rinpoche***.
